JUL 2026 — PRESENT · INDEPENDENT RESEARCH

Mathematical Formulation of Adaptive Step-Size Transformations

Investigating continuous, non-linear step-size transformations to control gradient scaling and momentum stability in high-dimensional optimization landscapes.

The broader objective is to explore a new point on the precision-versus-computational-cost trade-off of adaptive optimizers, with the potential to outperform established methods when tuned appropriately.

PCB Vibration Frequency Prediction

Benchmarked 12 ML models, including neural networks and transformer-based pretrained models, to predict the first-mode vibration frequency of PCBs from length, width, and copper percentage. ExtraTreesRegressor was selected as the most suitable model; future work includes Gaussian Process Regression and other Bayesian approaches.
